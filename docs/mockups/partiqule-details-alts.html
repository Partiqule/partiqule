Keep these for the gym.
For daily wear, there's something cleaner.
The why

Mostly the fabric. Nylon and elastane give Aligns the feel people love, but the same synthetics shed microplastics every wash and don't biodegrade.

Lululemon phased out PFAS from water-resistant coatings in 2024, which is a real upgrade over historical formulations.

For sweat sessions, totally fine. For daily leggings, cotton blends score cleaner with a little less snap-back.
