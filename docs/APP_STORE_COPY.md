## Description

Partiqule helps you decide what to buy.

Search a name, scan a barcode, or photograph a label. You get a score from 1 to 10. Lower is safer. The reason is under the number.

How a score is made

About 3,156 products have a hand-checked score. Clothing and personal care can be scored from the label by rules, with no model call. When a product is not in that library, a model reads the ingredients it can actually see. Partiqule does not invent a confident score for a label it cannot read.

What you can do

One score is free. No account and no card.

A subscription is $4.99 a month and covers the scores after that.

On the website, the first subscription is 7 days free and a card is required at the start. Cancel before the trial ends and the card is not charged.

On iPhone, the price and any free trial are what Apple shows on the payment sheet. Cancel in Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions.

Kids mode and pregnancy mode apply stricter rules to the same ingredients. They are shopping settings, not medical advice.

An account saves the scores you have already checked. When a cleaner option is already in the library, it can be listed next to the score.

What Partiqule is not

Partiqule is not a doctor and not a lab report. It is a shopping score.
